# Vrinda Store Annual Report 2022 – Excel Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data using Microsoft Excel. An interactive dashboard was developed to understand sales performance, customer demographics, order status and sales channels.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyse monthly sales and order trends.
- Compare sales between men and women.
- Understand customer age and gender distribution.
- Analyse order delivery and cancellation status.
- Identify the top-performing states.
- Compare sales across different shopping channels.

## 📈 Sample Business Insights

Based on the analysis of Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data, the following insights were identified:

- **Gender Analysis:** Women contribute approximately 65% of total purchases, compared to men.
- **Top Performing States:** Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are the top three contributing states, accounting for approximately 35% of sales.
- **Age Group Analysis:** Adults aged 30–49 years contribute approximately 50% of total purchases.
- **Sales Channels:** Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra are the leading sales channels, contributing approximately 80% of total sales.

## 💡 Business Recommendations & Final Conclusion

To improve Vrinda Store's sales performance, the following strategies are recommended:

1. **Target Women Customers:** Focus marketing campaigns on women customers, who contribute approximately 65% of purchases.
2. **Focus on the 30–49 Age Group:** Develop targeted offers and promotional campaigns for this major customer segment.
3. **Regional Marketing:** Focus on Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh to strengthen sales in the top-performing states.
4. **Optimize Online Channels:** Increase promotional activities, advertisements and discount coupons on Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra.

**Final Conclusion:** Vrinda Store can improve its sales by focusing on women customers aged 30–49, strengthening its presence in the top-performing states and using leading e-commerce platforms for targeted promotions.
